How to Get Your Dropbox Access Token:
  1. Go to developer.dropbox.com/apps
  2. Click Create app → Choose Scoped access → App folder.
  3. Name it (e.g. AliceVoiceKeepsakes).
  4. Under the Permissions tab, check files.content.write and click Submit.
  5. Under the Settings tab, click Generated access token.
  6. Copy and paste that token into the box below!

Allows guests to upload audio files directly to your Dropbox folder without extra clicks.
